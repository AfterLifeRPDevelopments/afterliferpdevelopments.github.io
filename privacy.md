# AfterLife RP – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

**What we access:** Basic profile info (display name, avatar) and the list of
public videos from the official AfterLife RP TikTok account only.

**What we do with it:** Post links to new public videos in the AfterLife RP
Discord server.

**What we store:** The TikTok login token and the IDs of videos already
posted, kept on our private server. Nothing else.

**What we don't do:** We don't access other TikTok users' data, and we don't
sell or share any data.

**Removing access:** The account owner can remove the app's access at any
time in TikTok under Settings → Security → Apps and services. We delete the
stored token on request.

Contact: AfterLife RP staff team, via our Discord server.
